Walmart+ Case StudyW+ Case Study // 2026

Membershipthat matters

Designing Walmart+ to make everyday essentials more accessible and affordable for millions of families.

  • My role: Product Design Director
  • Team: 20-25 designers, 3 Sr. Managers + cross functional partners
  • Key Result: Increased member LTV and their basket size by 25%

The challenge

Four things were pushing customers away.
Our goal was to pull them back.

Problem

  • Everyday shopping had gotten slow, costly, and complicated.

  • Delivery fees were confusing on every single grocery order.

  • Members were shopping and spending with Walmart less often.

  • Benefits were scattered across a dozen different services.

The opportunity

To pull them back we built a membership program based around saving time and money on everyday essentials.

  • One membership built to make everyday essentials affordable.

  • One consistent membership fee, with free delivery built in.

  • A subscription built to turn shoppers into engaged members.

  • One membership built to compete head-on with Amazon Prime.

My role

I launched and scaled Walmart’s first membership program, Walmart+.

As Walmart+’ founding design leader I was responsible for taking the program from 0-1 in 4 months which required strong shared principles, defining the cross-functional strategy and growing the team around real customer needs to build a program that could scale for years to come.

Shared Principles

Create a set of core principles to always keep members at the center of our experiences.

XF Strategy

Align XF partners to a simple framework to amplify strategic decision making.

Scale the journey

Build a high functioning design team with clear goals that mapped to the membership journey.

Where business goals met real needs

Designing the Membership Journey

Every stage of the journey was paired with a metric Walmart wanted to move. We had to show customer value at every stage to grow and maintain a strong membership offering.

Acquisition

How might we showcase the value of becoming a Walmart+ member?

Our focus was three-fold: a marketing page that led with customer value complete with every benefit’s impact on everyday needs, a sign-up flow that simplified choice, and a checkout flow that put the exact savings directly on the page to the customer can see that value in the moment.

Engagement

How might we get members to use benefits
more than once?

Members who tried one benefit tended to stop there, so we treated the second use as the real goal, worth rewarding immediately, and worth designing the whole hub around as each member's habits changed.

Retention

How might we continue to add benefits to increase the value of membership?

Retention wasn't going to be solved by stacking on more perks. The real question was how to make members feel the value of their membership again and again, so we built a recurring cadence of visible, unmissable moments that reminded people what they were already getting, rather than leaving that value to be discovered once and forgotten.

Impact to retention

75%

Retention for members who used 2+ benefits or offers

Loyalty

How might we celebrate a member to keep them
coming back for more?

Savings that happen quietly in the background don't register as savings at all. The strategy was to catch the exact moment a member's value crossed a threshold worth noticing, celebrate it plainly in the moment, and then give that value a permanent home a member could return to any time they needed a reminder of why they'd joined.

The impact of building a scalable, engaging
